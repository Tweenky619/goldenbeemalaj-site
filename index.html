What is a Goldback?

Goldbacks are the trustworthy, spendable gold currency of the future — real 24K gold designed for everyday transactions.

Protect Your Wealth

Exchanging your dollars into Goldbacks connects your wealth to the spot price of physical gold — disconnecting it from the devaluation that affects other currencies.
