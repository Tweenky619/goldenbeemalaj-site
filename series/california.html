What is a California Goldback?

A California Goldback is a physical note of real 24-karat gold, precisely vacuum-deposited onto a durable polymer substrate and printed with original California-themed artwork spanning Yosemite, Sutter's Mill, and Sonoma Valley. It's part of the Goldback currency system — designed to be spent, saved, or collected — and each note carries a unique serial number so its authenticity can be independently verified.
