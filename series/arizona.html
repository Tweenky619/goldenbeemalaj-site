What is a Arizona Goldback?

An Arizona Goldback is a physical note of real 24-karat gold, precisely vacuum-deposited onto a durable polymer substrate and printed with original Arizona-themed artwork honoring the Buffalo Soldiers (Valor), Mission San Xavier del Bac (Faith), and the Navajo Code Talkers (Liberty), set against Arizona's desert landscapes. It's part of the Goldback currency system, designed to be spent, saved, or collected, and each note carries a unique serial number so its authenticity can be independently verified.
