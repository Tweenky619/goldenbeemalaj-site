Nevada Goldback 2026 Series — New Artwork

The Silver State, redrawn — from the Pony Express to Hoover Dam.

Nevada was the second state to get its own Goldback series, back in 2020. In 2026 Goldback Inc. gave it a full rework: new artwork on every denomination, the latest security features, and three sizes Nevada never had before — the ¼, ½ and 2 Goldback.

Like every Goldback, each 2026 Nevada note is real 24-karat gold — not plated, not a token — vacuum-deposited onto a durable polymer note, with UV-reactive artwork you can check with a UV light.

Officially launched November 24, 2026 · ¼ through 50 Goldback · in stock now.

As San Diego's Official Authorized Goldback Distributor, Goldenbee MALAJ can set the new Nevada series aside for you. Collectors finishing a Nevada set: the 2020–2025 designs are now out of print, so both versions belong in a complete collection.
