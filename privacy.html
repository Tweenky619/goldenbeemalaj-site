Last updated: July 27, 2026

Goldenbee MALAJ Jewelry LLC ("Goldenbee MALAJ," "we," "us," or "our") operates goldenbeemalaj.com and app.goldenbeemalaj.com (together, the "Site"). This policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, and how we handle it.

Information We Collect

When you use our contact form, exchange request form, or scratch-card promotion, we collect the information you voluntarily submit, which may include:

  • Your name
  • Your email address and/or phone number
  • Details about what you're interested in (e.g. a specific Goldback series, an exchange amount, best time to contact you)

We do not collect payment card information, bank account numbers, or other sensitive financial account details through this Site. No purchase or exchange transaction is completed online — every request is followed up personally by phone or email before anything is finalized.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide solely to:

  • Respond to your inquiry or exchange request
  • Confirm pricing, availability, and scheduling for a pickup or shipment
  • Follow up regarding a scratch-card discount code you've claimed

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

Data Storage

Information submitted through our forms is stored in a secured database hosted on Cloudflare's infrastructure and is accessible only to the owners of Goldenbee MALAJ Jewelry LLC.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand general site traffic patterns (e.g. which pages are visited). Google Analytics may use cookies to collect anonymized usage data. You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking using your browser settings or available opt-out browser extensions.
